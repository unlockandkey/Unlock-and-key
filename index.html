Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common locksmith questions.

How much does a locksmith cost?

Prices depend on the type of lock, parts required, time of day and location. Contact Unlock & Key for a quote before deciding whether to go ahead.

Do you offer emergency lockout services?

I can help with lockouts subject to availability. Call or message me to explain your situation and check whether I can attend.

Can you replace uPVC door locks?

Yes, I can help with suitable uPVC lock and cylinder replacement jobs. Some faults involve the full locking mechanism, so photos can help me assess the problem.

Which areas do you cover?

I serve Birmingham and surrounding areas. Contact me with your postcode to confirm whether I cover your location and whether any travel charge applies.

Can I get a quote before booking?

Absolutely. Tell me what work you need, your postcode and the type of door or lock. Where possible, send photos through WhatsApp to help me assess the job.

Do you repair faulty door locks?

I can assess suitable lock and door problems, including some uPVC door issues. The repair required will depend on the fault and the parts involved.

Do you offer discounts?

Discounts may be available for students and older customers. Please ask when requesting your quote.
