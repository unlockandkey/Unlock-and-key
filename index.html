Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful details before you get in touch.

How much does a locksmith cost?

The price depends on your location, the lock, any parts needed and the work involved. Contact us with details for a quote before agreeing to the job.

Which areas do you cover?

Unlock & Key is based in the Kings Norton area of Birmingham and considers jobs across Birmingham and surrounding areas. Travel and availability may affect whether a particular job can be accepted.

Can I get a quote before booking?

Yes. Explain the problem and, where possible, provide the location, door type and a photo of the lock. This helps us discuss likely costs before arranging work.

Can you upgrade my existing locks?

In many cases, a compatible upgrade may be possible. The correct option depends on the door, existing mechanism and required level of security.

Do you offer discounts?

Discounts may be available for students and older customers. Ask when requesting your quote.
