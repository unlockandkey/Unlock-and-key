Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful answers before you get in touch.

How much does a locksmith cost?

The price depends on your location, the type of lock, the parts needed and the work involved. Contact me with the details for a quote before booking.

Can I get a quote before booking?

Yes. Tell me what has happened, where the job is and, if possible, send a photo of the lock or door. Some jobs may need an inspection before an exact price can be confirmed.

Which areas do you cover?

Unlock & Key is based in Birmingham and covers Birmingham and surrounding areas. Contact me with your postcode to check availability and any travel costs.

Can you replace my existing lock with a better one?

In many cases, yes. The suitable options depend on your door and existing mechanism. Send a photo or describe the lock and I can discuss what may be suitable.

Do you offer discounts?

Discounts may be available for students and older customers. Please mention this when asking for your quote.
